################################################################
WORK GUIDE
Folly II · Rusted Tower · Scorched Earth
################################################################

============================================================
I | Aether Rift
============================================================

Three moving antennas circle a central theremin. Wind changes their spacing and capacitance, shifting the oscillation frequency; the change becomes sound and structural vibration, then enters the sand bed below. The grains assemble and disperse as Chladni figures, giving a brief trace to an otherwise invisible sequence: electromagnetic change → frequency → sound → vibration → sand.

============================================================
II | Aether Tower and Collapsed Babel Tower
============================================================

Scorched-Earth Aether Tower is held by cables and separated members in a near-tensegrity relation, with a glass bead at its base acting as a “fictional anchor.” Swaying antennas, deforming members, and cable fatigue gradually consume tension; when the central structure falls, the tower is pushed toward the limit of losing its centre of gravity.

Collapsed Babel Tower inverts the tower into a grinding mechanism close to the ground. Its former steps become teeth that repeatedly pass over ground, concrete, and cables. Instead of destroying the tower in one blow, repetition slowly removes its support.

============================================================
III | How to Watch
============================================================

From sand, to tension, to abrasion, the three structures form a circuit that progressively loses stability. An invisible field first leaves a figure; that order enters the tower as tension, then is exhausted by the system's own motion. Collapse arrives as something the mechanism has been manufacturing all along.
