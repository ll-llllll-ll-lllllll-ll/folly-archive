################################################################
WORK GUIDE
Folly I · Sunken Ventricle
################################################################

============================================================
I | Deadwater Ventricle
============================================================

Sunken Ventricle compresses the site's continuing seepage into a large mechanical “ventricle.” Water inside provides support and pressure; as its level falls, the concrete core sinks, contracts, and cracks under its own weight. Rather than waiting for one decisive fracture, the work removes support incrementally, making nearly imperceptible settlement its slowest tempo.

============================================================
II | Rust-Blood Heart
============================================================

At the centre, a mechanical heart encloses rusted metal gathered from the ruin inside glass and keeps it in motion. Settlement below is carried through cables, pulleys, a resistance slide, and variable resistance into the drive system. As the structure approaches collapse, the heart's rhythm and load change; with every beat, the fragments collide and wear, so continued operation becomes another source of exhaustion.

============================================================
III | How to Watch
============================================================

Two speeds overlap: the ventricle loses support slowly, while the heart turns that change into faster, audible impacts. Collapse is treated less as an ending than as an ongoing mechanical process—the weaker the structure becomes, the more its sound is pushed forward.
